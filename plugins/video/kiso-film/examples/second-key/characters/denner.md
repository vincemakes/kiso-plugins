# Denner

## Appearance

**Deliberately not written.** Denner is heard and never seen in this episode:
the screenplay's rule is that no part of him is lit at any point, and the cliff
depends on it. There is nothing for an image model to be consistent about, and
writing an appearance here would invent a face the next episode has not chosen
yet.

He has a sheet all the same, because he has a **performance**, and the next
scene has to keep it the same.

## Wardrobe

None. He is not in frame.

## Performance

- **Speech.** A low baritone, unhurried. He states things: no rise at the end
  of a question, and no hurry to fill a silence. His first line is three words.
- **Position.** Close enough to be in the room rather than calling from it —
  the voice has no distance on it and no echo of a corridor.
- **What the camera should catch.** Nothing of him. The performance is carried
  entirely by the sound and by what it does to Ro's face in the shot she shares
  with it.

## Continuity

- Never lit, never in frame, in this episode.
- The voice is unhurried and close. If the next episode puts him in the light,
  it inherits that and not a face chosen here.

---

**Why this sheet exists at all.** The `characters` skill withdraws a sheet only
for a character with no performance either — a crowd, a name in a line. A voice
is not that: something downstream has to stay consistent about him, so he gets
a sheet with no *Appearance* section rather than no sheet.
