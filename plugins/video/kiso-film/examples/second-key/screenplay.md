# Second Key

## Scene 1 — INT. TOWER BLOCK, ELEVENTH-FLOOR LANDING — NIGHT

A door. 11B in brass. A key stands in the lock from the outside, and an orange
paper tag hangs off it, swinging slightly.

RO MERCER stops three steps away, tool roll under her arm. She does not reach
for the key.

She looks at the tag. The tag is orange. Her own ring, clipped to her belt
loop, carries eleven of them.

## Scene 2 — INT. LANDING — CONTINUOUS

Ro takes out her phone without looking away from the door. The job sheet is
open on it.

RO
Eleven B.

She thumbs the number at the top of the sheet. She lifts the phone to her ear.

Somewhere below, a lift starts. The phone rings out. She takes it from her ear
and looks at it.

## Scene 3 — INT. LANDING — CONTINUOUS

Ro crosses to the door. She turns the tag over without touching the key.

A number is written on it in pencil. The hand is hers.

She opens her job log on the phone and scrolls. She stops scrolling.

RO
No.

The entry reads the same address. The date is three weeks old. The initials at
the end of the line are hers.

## Scene 4 — INT. FLAT 11B — NIGHT

The key turns. The door gives inward onto a dark hall and a smell of new
plaster.

Ro puts her phone torch on. The flat is empty — no furniture, no curtains,
dust across the floor in one long drag mark.

The torch reaches the far wall. The wall is covered, corner to corner, in
small steel hooks. On every hook is a key. On every key is an orange tag.

Ro steps closer. She reads one, then the one beside it, then the one beside
that. The handwriting on all of them is hers.

## Scene 5 — INT. FLAT 11B, HALL — NIGHT

Ro turns toward the dark at the end of the hall.

DENNER (O.S.)
You're early.

Ro does not move. The torch does not reach him.

## Continuity

### Characters

**Ro Mercer** — thirty-eight, medium height, squarely built. Straight
dark-brown hair cut to the jaw, pushed behind one ear. Heavy eyebrows, a small
white scar through the left one. Navy work jacket with a reflective strip
across the back and a locksmith's shop name stitched in white on the left
chest; grey T-shirt; dark canvas trousers; a flat steel tape measure clipped
at the hip; a carabiner of keys with orange paper tags on the belt loop.
Speech: short and clipped, and she answers a different question when she does
not want the one she was asked.

**Denner** — heard, never seen, in this episode. A man's voice: a low baritone,
unhurried, close enough to be in the room rather than calling from it, with no
rise at the end of a question. Speech: he states things. No appearance is
established, deliberately — the episode ends before he is lit, and the next one
decides what he looks like.

### Places

**The eleventh-floor landing** — a short concrete landing, two flat doors, one
strip light working and a second that ticks and does not come on. Grey painted
walls to waist height, breeze-block above. Whenever we return it is the same
hour of the night and the same one light.

**Flat 11B** — empty. Bare floorboards with dust, no furniture, no curtains,
new plaster on the walls. The far wall carries small steel hooks from corner
to corner, a key on every hook, an orange paper tag on every key.

### Rules

- Ro checks her own pockets before she touches any lock. Every time. It is the
  first thing her hands do.
- The orange tags are the shop's, and the pencil handwriting on all of them is
  hers.
- The only light in Scenes 4 and 5 is Ro's phone torch and what comes in from
  the landing behind her.
- Denner is never in frame in this episode. No part of him is lit.
